# REUSABLE AI CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA) GENERATOR

## ROLE

Act as an expert conversion copywriter and digital marketing strategist.

Your task is to create persuasive, customer-focused call-to-action content for a local business website.

## INPUT VARIABLES

Business Name: Coffee Corner

Business Type: Cafe

Location: Kolkata, India (Fictional Demo Business)

Target Audience: College Students, Working Professionals, Families and Friends

Products or Services:
- Coffee
- Tea and Beverages
- Snacks
- Desserts
- Dine-in Experience
- Takeaway Service
- Group Gatherings
- Special Occasions

Unique Selling Proposition: Fresh beverages, delicious snacks, desserts, and a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere.

Brand Tone: Friendly, Professional and Confident

Primary Goal: Encourage website visitors to explore the menu, visit the cafe, and contact the business.

## OBJECTIVE

Generate multiple effective CTA sections that encourage website visitors to take meaningful actions.

The CTAs should be clear, engaging, action-oriented, and relevant to the business.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT STRUCTURE

### 1. Homepage Hero CTA
- CTA headline
- Supporting sentence
- Primary CTA button
- Secondary CTA button

### 2. Menu Exploration CTA
- Headline
- Short description
- CTA button

### 3. Service-Specific CTAs

Create separate CTAs for:
- Dine-in Experience
- Takeaway Service
- Group Gatherings
- Special Occasions

Each CTA must include a headline, supporting sentence, and button text.

### 4. Trust-Building CTA

Create a CTA that encourages customers to choose the business by highlighting its welcoming atmosphere, product variety, and customer experience.

### 5. Location-Based CTA

Create a CTA encouraging local customers to visit the business.

Do not invent an exact address, directions, or map link.

### 6. Urgency-Based CTA

Create a natural CTA that encourages customers to take action without using fake scarcity, false deadlines, or misleading offers.

### 7. Final Website CTA
- Strong closing headline
- Supporting sentence
- Primary CTA button
- Secondary CTA button

## CTA STRATEGY ANALYSIS

After generating the CTAs, explain:
- Which CTA should be used on the homepage?
- Which CTA is best for increasing menu exploration?
- Which CTA is best for encouraging visits?
- Which CTA is suitable for the contact page?
- Why are the selected CTAs effective?

## QUALITY RULES

- Use short, clear, action-oriented button text.
- Keep CTA headlines engaging and benefit-driven.
- Avoid generic phrases when a business-specific CTA is possible.
- Do not invent discounts, promotions, prices, phone numbers, or booking links.
- Do not use misleading urgency or fake scarcity.
- Ensure each CTA matches the section where it will appear.
- Avoid repeating identical CTA wording unnecessarily.

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

- Use clear headings.
- Separate each CTA section.
- Present button text clearly.
- Include a short strategy explanation.
- Make all content suitable for direct website implementation.

Generate the complete CTA content now.
