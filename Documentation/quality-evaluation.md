# QUALITY EVALUATION
## AI Website Copy Generator for Local Businesses

### 1. Introduction

This document evaluates the AI-generated website copy created for the fictional demo business Coffee Corner.

Four content categories were evaluated:
1. Homepage Copy
2. Services Page Copy
3. Call-to-Action Content
4. Brand Tone Adaptation

The evaluation focuses on clarity, relevance, structure, customer engagement, and accuracy.

### 2. Evaluation Criteria

The following criteria were used to review the generated content.

| Criteria | Evaluation Focus |
|---|---|
| Clarity | Is the content easy to understand? |
| Relevance | Does the content match the business? |
| Structure | Is the content properly organized? |
| Customer Focus | Does it communicate customer benefits? |
| CTA Effectiveness | Does it encourage meaningful action? |
| Accuracy | Does it avoid unsupported business claims? |
| Tone Consistency | Does the language match the requested tone? |

### 3. Generated Content Evaluation

#### A. Homepage Copy

**Strengths:**
- Clear business introduction.
- Attractive headline and subheadline.
- Highlights products and customer benefits.
- Includes relevant CTA sections.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Some descriptions can be made more concise.
- Actual business details should be verified before publishing.

**Overall Assessment:** Good structure and customer-focused messaging.

#### B. Services Page Copy

**Strengths:**
- Services are separated into clear sections.
- Each service explains customer benefits.
- Includes service-specific CTAs.
- Suitable for a cafe website.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Service availability needs verification.
- Operational details should not be assumed.

**Overall Assessment:** Well-organized and informative content.

#### C. CTA Content

**Strengths:**
- Includes different CTA types.
- Button text is simple and action-oriented.
- CTAs are relevant to different website sections.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- CTA destinations must be connected to actual website pages.
- Location and contact details must be verified.

**Overall Assessment:** Clear and conversion-focused CTA suggestions.

#### D. Tone Adaptation

**Strengths:**
- Three clearly different communication styles.
- Includes tone comparison tables.
- Provides target audience recommendations.
- Suggests a consistent brand voice.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Tone recommendations should be tested with actual customers.
- Suggested brand voice percentages are strategic estimates.

**Overall Assessment:** Useful brand communication and tone variation.

### 4. Overall Quality Summary

| Content Category | Main Strength |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Clear business introduction |
| Services | Organized service descriptions |
| CTA | Action-oriented messaging |
| Tone Adaptation | Different brand communication styles |

### 5. Improvements Made Through Prompt Engineering

The prompts were designed with:
- Clear role definitions.
- Business-specific input variables.
- Structured output requirements.
- Brand tone instructions.
- Quality and accuracy rules.
- Restrictions against invented information.

These improvements help make the generated content more consistent and usable.

### 6. Limitations

- Coffee Corner is a fictional demo business.
- Business services and operational details are not independently verified.
- Generated copy requires human review before real-world publication.
- CTA links and contact information need actual configuration.
- AI-generated content may require further editing for specific businesses.

### 7. Final Conclusion

The project demonstrates that structured prompts can generate useful website copy for different business requirements.

Using clear instructions, relevant business information, and quality evaluation criteria helps improve the usefulness and consistency of AI-generated content.
