## 7. FINAL BRAND TONE RECOMMENDATION

### Recommended Primary Tone: Friendly

### Supporting Tone: Professional

### Conversion Tone: Confident

Coffee Corner should primarily communicate in a friendly and welcoming voice. Professional language should support clear communication, while confident messaging should encourage customers to explore the menu and visit the cafe.

### Suggested Brand Voice Distribution

- Friendly: 70%
- Professional: 20%
- Confident: 10%

### Recommended Website Application

1. Homepage: Friendly tone to create a welcoming first impression.
2. About Us Page: Friendly + Professional to build connection and credibility.
3. Services Page: Friendly + Professional to explain services clearly.
4. Menu Page: Confident + Friendly to encourage menu exploration.
5. CTA Sections: Confident to encourage customer action.
6. Contact Page: Professional + Friendly to make enquiries comfortable.

### Brand Voice Statement

"Coffee Corner is a friendly and welcoming cafe where freshly prepared beverages, delicious snacks, and desserts come together to create enjoyable moments. We communicate with warmth, keep things simple, and invite everyone to enjoy a comfortable cafe experience."

Note: Coffee Corner is a fictional demo business. The suggested tone distribution is a strategic recommendation, not a research-backed measurement.
